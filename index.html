About LPM-FSI Group

Advancing Computational Thermo-Fluid-Structure Interaction with Applications to Multiphysics Systems

The LPM-FSI Group develops analytical, numerical, and computational framework for the modeling, simulation, and analysis of coupled multiphysics systems governed by interacting fluid, structural, thermal, and electrical domains. Its extended rigid-body lumped-mass framework represents distributed structural and thermal properties through high-fidelity discrete surrogate models that support integration with fluid and electrical subsystems.

The framework combines governing-equation-based modeling, computaitonal fluid dynamics, structural dynamics, analytical stability analysis, and high-performance computing to investigate aerodynamic and hydrodynamic loading, thermal effects, vibration, stability, and post-integration behavior. Applications inlcude renewable energy, offshore infrastructure, and propulsion system in aerial, naval,and marine vehicles.

JOIN LPM-FSI GROUP

We welcome graduate students from multiple engineering disciplines interested in analytical, numerical, and computational multiphysics research.

Mechnical Engineering - Dynamic-system modeling, 2D and 3D dynamic modeling, multibody dynamics, linear and nonlinear analytical analysis, mechanical vibration, and parametric instabilities.

Computational Engineering - High-fidelity CFD and FEA simulations.

Aerospace Engineering - Aerodynamic-force prediction, aeroelasticity, and flutter analysis.

Electrical Engineering - Power generation, power transmission, power-grid modeling and integration, grid stability, control systems, and electromechanical coupling.

Civil Engineering - Structural and thermal discretization, lumped-paramter modeling, structural dynamics, and base excitation.

Ocean Engineering - Hydrodynamic loading, added-mass effects, and free vibration and wave excitation of floating structures.
